      What is this?

      On July 21, 2026, about fifty people in Anchorage each wrote a hope, a fear, or a question about AI on an index card and pinned it to a board. This is thirty-eight of those cards, in the original handwriting.

      It was built on August 4, 2026 at the Claude Impact Lab at Alaska Pacific University, by a team who met that afternoon:

      Mary · Sam · Anna · Teresa · Cory · Megan · Aaron

      It does not answer the questions. It lays out the tradeoffs, hands you three questions to take away, and tells you where it might be wrong. That's deliberate — one of the cards on this wall asks how we make these tools resist us, to make us stronger rather than making us more comfortable. We took that seriously.

      Nothing you tap is stored or sent anywhere. This page works with the network unplugged.
