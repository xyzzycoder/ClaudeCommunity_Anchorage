# Anchorage Claude Conversation — Table Worksheets, Batch 1

Event: Claude Conversation, "What is AI doing to me, my kids, my community?" (Anchorage, July 21, 2026)

Printed worksheet template (Anthropic-branded, orange starburst logo top-right on each cover page). Standard header/prompts appear on every worksheet:
- Title: **What is AI doing to me, my kids, my community?**
- Intro: *"Go around the table first — your name, what brought you tonight, and one place AI has touched your home or neighborhood."*
- 01 **What does this mean for us, here?** — *What's changing in our homes, schools, and streets? Whose story surprised you?*
- 02 **What are we hoping for — and what are we worried about?** — *Be specific: not AI in general, but this street, this classroom, this job.* (HOPES / WORRIES columns)
- 03 **What can we do about it?** — *Think at every scale — what one person could try this week, or what the whole city could build together.*
- 04 **What stands in our way?** — *Time, money, know-how, permission — which one weighs most here?*
- 05 **Where do we start?** — *Of everything said tonight — what's small enough to start, and big enough to matter?*

Note: scans 1–2 appear to be the front and back pages of one table's worksheet; scans 3–4 appear to be front and back of another table's worksheet; scan 5 is a third table's page 1.
