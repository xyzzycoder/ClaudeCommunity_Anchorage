## The brief

Teams build things that help Alaskans **learn this technology, teach it, and think
clearly around it**. Named audiences:

- Parents figuring out what to tell their kids
- Teachers and school staff
- Anyone who wants to use these tools well and understand what's behind them

*What gets built is up to the teams.*

## Format notes

- Teams are formed **ahead of time**; team + table number sent before the day.
- Non-developers are explicitly expected. "Claude Code does the heavy lifting, and
  every team needs people who know the problem, know how things actually work here,
  and ask good questions."
- Guests arrive at 5:30 for dinner and demos — **the demo audience is broader than
  the builders.** Assume non-technical viewers.

## Implications for us

- Scope for a ~4-hour build with a mixed-skill team of strangers.
- Local specificity is the differentiator — "for Alaskans" was in the brief twice.
- The demo is to a room that includes people who just walked in. Lead with the
  problem, not the stack.
