# What Worked

Capture live, not at 7:00 PM. Each entry: what we did, and *why it worked* — the
why is the transferable part.

## Process

- **Transcribing the prior session's card wall before choosing a project.** The
  event page compressed two hours into four words ("education and access"). The
  actual cards showed something different — six of eleven closing cards were about
  *community involvement*, and the most concrete pain was a parent asking what to
  say to their kids. Reading the primary source changed the candidate list.

## Tooling

- **Persisting pasted material to disk immediately.** Screenshots and photos pasted
  into a session are the project's source material; they vanish with the session
  otherwise.
