## What we'd tell a team starting from scratch

Seeded from the July 21 material; extend with what today teaches us.

1. **Read the prior session's raw cards, not the summary.** The summary said
   "education and access." The cards said the community also wanted to be *asked*.
2. **The concrete pain is a parent with a 9-, 12-, and 15-year-old.** If you need
   a user, that person is real and was in the room.
3. **"Don't add work to teachers" is a hard constraint**, not a nice-to-have. It
   was the framing of the schools question.
4. **Alaska Native data and knowledge sovereignty came up repeatedly and
   specifically.** Don't build something that speaks for communities that weren't
   consulted; build something that invites them in.
